CUESNAPI PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy

Version 1.0 · Effective September 7, 2026

Data handled

CueSnapi handles billiards table photos you select or capture, ball layouts, layout names and notes, and app settings to provide its features.

Photo access

Photo library and camera access are used only to load a photo you choose or take one. Photos are analyzed on the device. CueSnapi does not automatically send them to an external server.

Storage

In Version 1.0, layouts and settings are generally stored on your device. A dedicated account and automatic cloud sync are not provided.

Purchases

CueSnapi Pro purchases and restores are handled by Apple through StoreKit and the App Store. CueSnapi does not receive or store payment card details.

Advertising and tracking

Version 1.0 does not track users for advertising. We do not sell or rent your app data.

Deletion

You can delete saved layouts in the app. Deleting the app may also delete its on-device data.

Contact

For privacy questions, email cuescore.apps@gmail.com.
